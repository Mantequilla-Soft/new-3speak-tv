Discussion beside the video. The right column is the conversation, not "Up next". Comments with timestamps sit next to the player like a room you're in.
An action dock on the player. Vote, comment, share, save, tip and react are a vertical tile rail on the player's edge. No like/dislike pill under the title.
Community context. A strip above the player shows which community this was posted in, with Join. The creator card floats on the video.
Chapters, then what's next. Chapter cards run under the player. Up next becomes a shelf from the same playlist or community, plus a card on the player at the end.
