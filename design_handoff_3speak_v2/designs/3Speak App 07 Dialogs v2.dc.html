07Dialogs v2: a 3Speak dialog style, less like YouTube
Side sheets, not pop-ups. On desktop, dialogs slide in from the right as a full-height sheet with the page still visible and blurred. On mobile they're bottom sheets.
Context first. Every sheet opens with a card showing what you're acting on (the video, creator or amount), so you never lose your place.
Tiles, not lists. Choices are big icon tiles in a grid instead of rows of radio buttons and checkboxes.
Hive-native. Share leads with Reblog and Snap, and external apps come second. The main action is a full-width bar pinned to the bottom.
