20Consistency pass 1: Home, Channel and Community in v2
1 · Two colours. Red is for the main action and for votes. Cyan marks community, time and "who's here". Everything else is ink and grey.
2 · Tiles, not pills. Actions are 64px tiles with an icon and a label. Choices are tiles with a 2px ring when picked.
3 · Segmented tabs. Tab sets sit in a rounded tray, and the active tab is a raised chip. No underlines.
4 · Context strip. Any content page opens with a cyan strip saying where you are: the community, playlist or tag.
5 · Main action. One full-width button with the label on the left and the icon on the right. Dialogs open as side sheets.
