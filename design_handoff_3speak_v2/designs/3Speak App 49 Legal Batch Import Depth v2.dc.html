How 3Speak handles your data
Last updated: 14 July 2026
Read this first: 3Speak runs on a public blockchain
When you post, comment, vote or reshare, it is signed with your own Hive keys and broadcast to the Hive blockchain, a public, permanent, decentralised ledger.
This content cannot be deleted, not by us, not by you, not by anyone. Deleting your 3Speak account removes our copies, but the blockchain records remain public forever.
